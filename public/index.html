    Help & Support

    Getting started

    Where do I get my keys?

    A Gemini key is free from Google AI Studio (link in the Your Keys card). An ElevenLabs key comes from your ElevenLabs profile under API keys. Both are yours: VlogMe Free never sees or stores them.

    Is my footage private?

    Your clips stay on your device. A small low-resolution, silent copy of each clip is sent to Google Gemini so it can pick the best moments, and your script text is sent to ElevenLabs for the voice, both under your own accounts.

    Why is rendering slow?

    The video is built inside your browser, so speed depends on your device. Shorter clips, fewer clips and a shorter target length render faster. Keep this tab open while it works.

    My phone clip would not load

    Some phone videos use HEVC. Convert the clip to H.264 MP4 and try again.
