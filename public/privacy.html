Privacy Policy

VlogMe Free · Last updated: November 15, 2026

VlogMe Free (“the app”) is a free tool, operated by Zero Touch AI, that helps you turn your own video clips into a narrated video. This page explains what information the app uses and where it goes. The short version: the app runs in your web browser, and we do not run a server or database that stores your data.

What we do not do

  • We do not store your videos, scripts, audio or API keys on any server we control.
  • We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.
  • We do not have a user database. There is no account on our side to delete.

Information the app uses

  • Google sign-in. When you choose “Continue with Google” the app receives your name, email address, profile photo and Google account ID. These are shown in the app and kept in your own browser’s local storage so you stay signed in on that device. They are not sent to us.
  • Saved settings in your Google Drive. The app asks for access to a hidden, app-only folder in your Google Drive (the drive.appdata permission). It stores one small file there containing your preferences: video style, format, tone, length, voice choice and AI model name. The app cannot see, read or change any of your other Google Drive files, and the folder is not visible in your Drive. Your API keys are never saved there.
  • Your video clips. Clips stay on your device. They are processed inside your browser.
  • Your API keys. The Gemini and ElevenLabs keys you enter are used only by your browser to contact those services. They are kept for the current browser session, or on your device if you tick “Remember my keys”. Signing out clears them.

Services your browser talks to

To make a video, your browser sends data directly to these services, using your own accounts and keys. We do not see this traffic, and each service’s own terms and privacy policy apply.

  • Google Gemini receives a small, silent, low-resolution copy of each clip you analyze, plus your notes and the script request.
  • ElevenLabs receives the narration text and returns the voice audio.
  • Google provides sign-in and Google Drive storage for your settings, and Google Fonts for the page typeface (which means Google receives your IP address when the page loads).
  • Vercel hosts the website and, like most web hosts, may keep standard server logs such as IP address and request time.

Google user data

VlogMe Free’s use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to sign you in and to save and load your settings. It is not shared with third parties, not used for advertising, and not read by people.

Affiliate links

Some links in the app are affiliate links. If you sign up or upgrade through them, the project may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Those partner sites have their own cookies and privacy policies.

Your choices

  • Sign out in the app to clear your keys and sign-in from that browser.
  • To remove the app’s access to your Google account and delete the settings file it stored, visit your Google account’s connections page, select VlogMe Free, and choose to delete its connection and data.
  • You can also clear this site’s data in your browser settings at any time.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.

Contact
